Places to Visit near Dalhousie and Banikhet

Use Hotel Nature Valley in Banikhet as a peaceful base to visit Dalhousie, Khajjiar, Kalatop Wildlife Sanctuary, Dainkund Peak and Dharamshala.

~8 km (21 min drive)

Dalhousie

Colonial-era churches, Gandhi Chowk, Mall Road walks and sweeping views across five Himalayan hills.

26.4–28.6 km (~1 hr 10 min)

Khajjiar

Known as the "Mini Switzerland of India" — a serene high-altitude meadow ringed by dense deodar forests.

~99 km (Day trip)

Dharamshala & McLeod Ganj

Home to Tibetan monasteries, mountain cafes, HPCA Cricket Stadium and the Triund trailhead.
