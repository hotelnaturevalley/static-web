Celebrations

Destination Weddings near Dalhousie

Host your wedding functions amidst the Himalayan foothills in Banikhet — with indoor banquet halls, scenic outdoor lawns, full in-house catering, and 65 rooms on site for your guests.

The Venue

Banquet Hall, Lawns & 65 Guest Rooms Under One Roof

Located on Dalhousie Road in Surkhigala, Banikhet — just 500 metres from Banikhet bus stand and 8 km before Dalhousie town — Hotel Nature Valley makes it easy for outstation family and guests to arrive, park comfortably and celebrate without city traffic.

  • Banquet Hall & Open Lawns: Indoor banquet space for ceremonies and sangeet nights, plus a green terrace lawn with valley views.
  • 65 Rooms On Site: Deluxe, Super Deluxe and Luxury rooms so both families stay comfortably together on the property.
  • In-House Catering & Event Support: Custom menus featuring Himachali, North Indian and continental dishes.
  • Ample Guest Parking: Convenient highway access from Pathankot (73.2 km) with dedicated parking.
Why Banikhet

What Wedding Families Appreciate

Unlike crowded Mall Road hotels in central Dalhousie where coaches and cars struggle to park, Banikhet sits right along the approach road from Pathankot. Guests have open terrace spaces to mingle, comfortable indoor amenities including our glass-roofed swimming pool, and quick access to local sightseeing in Dalhousie and Khajjiar between functions.
