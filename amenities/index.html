On the Property

Amenities at Hotel Nature Valley

From a glass-roofed swimming pool open across seasons to in-house dining, gym, terrace seating, banquet hall and free parking in Banikhet, near Dalhousie.

Wellness & Leisure

Glass-Roofed Swimming Pool & Mountain Terrace

Our swimming pool features mosaic-tiled edges beneath a covered glass roof, allowing guests to enjoy a swim even when the hillside air turns cool in Banikhet.

Outdoors, our spacious lawn terrace and hillside seating overlook the green slopes of the Dhauladhar foothills — ideal for morning tea or quiet evening sunsets.

In-House Dining

Multi-Cuisine Restaurant & Meal Plans

Our on-site dining hall serves fresh North Indian, Himachali and continental meals prepared in-house — consistently praised in guest reviews for light, home-style cooking. Guests can book room-only or include breakfast, lunch and dinner plans directly while reserving.

Fitness Centre

On-site gym equipment so you can maintain your workout routine while travelling.

Conference Hall

Dedicated meeting space for corporate off-sites and business gatherings.

Banquet Hall & Lawns

Spacious celebration spaces for destination weddings and family events.

Ample Parking & WiFi

Convenient on-site parking off Dalhousie Road and complimentary WiFi for guests.
